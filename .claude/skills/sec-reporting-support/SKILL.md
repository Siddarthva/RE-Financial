# SEC Reporting Support

## Goal
Assist with data collection, tie-outs, variance explanations, and draft disclosure support. Do not represent output as a filed report or legal/accounting conclusion.

## Workflow
1. Confirm the issuer, reporting period, filing type, reporting framework, source statements, and approved reporting instructions.
2. Build a source inventory and map each proposed metric or narrative to a source and calculation.
3. Reconcile key financial figures to approved statements and supporting schedules.
4. Draft factual, neutral variance commentary using only substantiated facts.
5. Mark missing support, unresolved tie-outs, inconsistent definitions, and review dependencies.
6. Preserve a traceability matrix from draft output to source statement, worksheet/cell or report, calculation, and reviewer.

## Output
- Scope and assumptions
- Metric/source traceability table
- Tie-out and exception list
- Draft narrative clearly labelled `FOR REVIEW — NOT FILE-READY`
- Required accountant/legal reviewer questions

## Guardrails
- Never invent disclosures, materiality judgments, risk factors, non-GAAP adjustments, or management explanations.
- Do not claim SEC compliance or filing readiness.
- Do not give legal advice or replace review by the issuer's qualified finance, accounting, and legal professionals.
- Do not upload or transmit client data to an external service unless the approved environment permits it.
