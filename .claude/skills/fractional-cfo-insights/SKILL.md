# Fractional CFO Insights

## Goal
Create a decision-support brief from supplied financial evidence. This is not a substitute for the client's CFO, accountant, lawyer, tax adviser, or investment adviser.

## Analyze when data exists
- Revenue growth and concentration
- Gross and operating margins
- Operating expense run rate
- Profitability and break-even indicators
- Cash balance, cash movement, and runway only when cash-flow/forecast inputs support it
- Actual vs budget/forecast
- Key assumptions, risks, and unanswered questions

## Cash runway
If a supported monthly net cash burn is available and positive:
`estimated runway months = available unrestricted cash / monthly net cash burn`.
State the period and methodology used. If cash burn is zero/negative, irregular, or forecast data is absent, do not force a runway estimate; explain the limitation.

## Workflow
1. State entity, period, currency, accounting basis, and source coverage.
2. Use validated metrics from deterministic analysis.
3. Separate historical actuals, management forecasts, and scenario assumptions.
4. Highlight no more than five decision-relevant observations, each with supporting figures.
5. State risks, uncertainties, and questions that management should resolve.
6. Present scenario options only when assumptions are explicit.

## Output
- Decision brief (short)
- KPI snapshot with definitions
- Key movements and evidence
- Forecast assumptions and sensitivity (if supplied)
- Risks / watch items
- Decisions and questions for management

## Guardrails
Do not claim that a company is healthy, distressed, solvent, or insolvent from a few ratios alone. Do not invent forecast inputs, debt terms, funding availability, or customer economics.
